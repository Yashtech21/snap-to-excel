# Snap to Excel Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 29, 2026**

Snap to Excel is a Chrome extension that captures a webpage and saves it to an Excel workbook selected by the user. The user can select an `.xlsx` file on their device for offline use or choose a OneDrive/SharePoint workbook.

## Information the extension handles

When you request a capture, the extension processes a screenshot of the visible page or selected area and may read the page title and URL. Depending on your settings, it also records the timestamp and file name. A screenshot can contain personal or sensitive information visible in the selected area, so review the page before capturing it.

The extension stores settings and screenshots awaiting a save in Chrome on your device. For local workbook mode, the screenshot image and selected page details are written to the `.xlsx` file you chose. Chrome grants access only to the file you select. Pending captures are retained until saved or discarded. You can discard pending captures and clear locally stored extension data in Settings.

For optional OneDrive/SharePoint mode, the extension stores Microsoft sign-in information locally and sends screenshots and selected page details to the Microsoft location and workbook you choose through Microsoft Graph. Microsoft processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Sharing and sale

Snap to Excel does not sell personal information. It does not send screenshots or page details to the extension developer, advertising networks, or analytics services. In local workbook mode, captures stay on your device. In cloud mode, information is shared with Microsoft only as needed to complete the save operation you request.

## Your choices and deletion

You choose when and where to capture, whether to include the page title, URL, or timestamp, and which local or cloud workbook to use. You can discard queued captures and clear locally stored extension data from Settings. To remove information already saved, delete it from your local workbook or from your OneDrive/SharePoint workbook.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the extension's behavior changes. The current version is available in the Snap to Excel GitHub repository. For privacy questions, open an issue at: https://github.com/Yashtech21/snap-to-excel/issues
